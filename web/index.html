Sessions are banners

  • Waving banner: busy on campaign
  • Beacon fire on a hill: waiting on you; a "?" above it for a question, a padlock for a permission prompt
  • Banner still, low candle: idle
  • Lowered grey banner: offline
  • Grey smoke: stalled (a banner busy, or a scout quiet, with nothing new for 5 min)
  • A gold flash at the finial: a new tool call

What a banner is doing

  • Anvil and sparks: editing
  • Catapult: running commands
  • Scroll on a lectern: reading
  • Raven to the map edge: web
  • Horn and gold ripples: delegating
  • Furled banner, pale motes: thinking
  • War drum: another tool

Subagents are scouts

  • Riding the dotted path: active (pennant = action colour)
  • Tent and campfire at the cross: resting
  • Rider's cloak and saddle cloth: their lead's banner colour
  • A small plate under a scout: it works on its own git branch

Places are regions

  • Each repo is a region; its border glows when a banner there needs you
  • A stone keep grows towers as the region's tool calls mount
